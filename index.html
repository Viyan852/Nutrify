The Problem

Mission 2: The Nutrition & Waste Crisis

Billions lack balanced nutrition while a third of all food is wasted. Nutrify tackles both — on the same plate.

🍽️

Hidden Hunger

Over 2 billion people survive on calories but miss the protein, iron and vitamins their bodies need to grow and focus.

🗑️

Food Waste

One-third of all food produced is thrown away — wasting the water, land and energy that made it, and fueling emissions.

🐝

Our Answer

Personalized meals built around what you already have, so good nutrition and zero waste become the same daily habit.

7-Day Challenge Guide

  1. Register your school and set a start date
  2. Each student creates a Nutrify profile
  3. Track balanced meals, water and leftovers daily
  4. Log before/after nutrition scores in Community Impact
  5. Celebrate completion and submit results to 1M1B

Replication Checklist
